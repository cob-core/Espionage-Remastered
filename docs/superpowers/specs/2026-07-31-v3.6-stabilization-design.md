# v3.6 Stabilization — Design Spec

Date: 2026-07-31 · Status: approved by user (two-release plan; this is release 1 of 2)

## Goal

Ship every fix that makes currently live content behave as designed, plus hygiene and
metadata. No new mechanics. v4.0 (separate spec, brainstormed after v3.6 ships) finishes
the half-done features: the four `TODO/` actions, AfL/Sow Discontent journal entries,
catalyst wiring, election auto-end, AI party choice.

## Approved decisions

1. **`TODO/` actions stay enabled** in v3.6. Removing them would break in-flight saves
   holding active pacts of those types; v4.0 finishes them. v3.6 fixes only their
   outright defects.
2. **`counter_piracy` is removed, not defined.** The 1.13 naval piracy mechanics make the
   mod's counter-piracy accounting redundant. General principle (applies forward, incl.
   the v4.0 Fund Piracy rework): **features made redundant by newer vanilla mechanics are
   removed, not maintained.**
3. `piracy_cost_multiplier` gets a real GDP-scaled definition consistent with the
   documented cost tiers (small tier), so its five existing call sites work unchanged.
   Flagged in changelog as rebalanced (original author intent unrecoverable).
